A product person.
A people person.

I work at the intersection of product strategy, data science, and AI. At Posit, I help customers and cloud partners make technical tools easier to adopt and more valuable in practice.

Over more than a decade in the field, I’ve learned that strong product work starts with listening closely. Technology should make us more human, not less.

My work spans 0-to-1 AI tools, enterprise reporting, and partnerships with AWS, Azure, GCP, Databricks, and Snowflake. I bring a technical background in Python, R, and SQL, and a teacher’s instinct for making complex ideas clear.
