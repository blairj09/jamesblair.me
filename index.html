About Me

I work at the intersection of product strategy, data science, and AI. At Posit (formerly RStudio), I partner with customers and cloud platforms to make technical tools easier to adopt and more valuable in practice. Over more than a decade in the field, I’ve learned that strong product work starts with listening closely, then creating enough space for people to do their best thinking.

Work

I lead product work across cloud partnerships, enterprise integrations, and AI-enabled workflows. That has included building targeted demo tools that cut preparation time by 90%, maintaining close customer relationships, and helping partners deliver better data science experiences. I also speak and teach regularly through conference talks, keynotes, and hands-on technical workshops.

Home

I’m based in Eagle Mountain, Utah with my wife and our five kids. Away from work, family time usually means road trips, time near the water, games, backyard barbecues, and a healthy amount of cheering for American Ninja Warrior.

Hobbies

When I'm not working on product strategy or speaking at conferences, you can find me enjoying Utah's majestic scenery from a bicycle. I'm a mediocre road racer (Cat 4) with my main season target being LoToJa, which I've completed 3 times. One of my biggest goals is to finish in under 9.5 hours. My favorite road ride is Arches National Park, and my favorite mountain bike ride is anything around Lake Tahoe. I also run Landform Labs, a small 3D printing business focused on creating geographical features and custom pieces for bike rides, runs, and hikes.
